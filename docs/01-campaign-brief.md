# 01 · Campaign brief

## The business problem

Running is where Nike's challengers have grown fastest. Deckers' Hoka brand reached about **$2.2 billion** in fiscal 2025 sales, up roughly 24% (Zacks, 2025), and On grew net sales **29%** to CHF 2.32 billion in 2024 (On Holding, 2025). Both won runners with a clear product story: cushioning for Hoka, Swiss engineering and design for On.

Nike has named running one of its priorities since Elliott Hill became CEO in October 2024 and refocused the company on sport (Fortune, 2025). The question for this campaign: **where can Nike win new runners that Hoka and On aren't built to reach?**

## The cultural insight

Running has become social. Strava's 2024 Year in Sport report found a **59% increase** in running club participation, and **66% of Gen Z** respondents said they made new friends through a fitness group that year (Strava, 2024, via Athletech News).

For a lot of new runners, the club comes first and the running comes second. They don't join to get faster. They join to have somewhere to be on a Tuesday night.

**Insight:** *The hardest part of running isn't the miles. It's showing up the first time.*

## The idea

**Show Up.** A campaign that celebrates the run club, not the podium: the first group run, the nervous jog to the meeting point, the coffee afterwards. Nike's role is to make the first time easier, and the product that goes with it is the **Pegasus**, Nike's everyday running shoe, positioned as the shoe for every pace in the group.

## Objectives

| Objective | Measure | Target (6 months) |
|---|---|---|
| Recruit new runners into Nike's ecosystem | New Nike Run Club (NRC) app members aged 18–24 | +25% vs. same period last year |
| Make Nike the brand of the run club | Share of voice in run club social content (#runclub) | #1 among running brands |
| Sell the everyday shoe | Pegasus unit sales, US, 18–24 buyers | +15% |
| Keep them coming back | NRC members joining 3+ group runs in 60 days | 35% |

Targets are planning assumptions for the concept, not Nike data.

## Mandatories

- Real run clubs and real members, no casting of elite athletes as "regular" runners.
- Every pace is shown, including walk breaks.
- Safety at night: lights, routes and group rules shown on screen.
